## Levels

- **Foundations:** Explain the core concept.
- **Applied:** Use the concept in a practical situation.
- **Advanced scenarios:** Diagnose behavior or reason about trade-offs.

## How to use this bank

Pick a role-relevant topic. Answer out loud before opening the model answer. For scenario questions, state your assumptions, compare two options, and explain how you would verify the result. Revisit missed questions rather than rereading the entire list.

## Go from reading to practice with Mentaro

**Study against a real goal.** [Explore the Mentaro preparation program](https://www.mentaro.app/product) and build a plan around your target role, seniority, stack, and interview date. Adjust which topics matter and how deeply to study them.

**Recall first, then repeat at the right interval.** [Try the interactive demo](https://www.mentaro.app/demo). Answer a card before looking for help, rate your recall, and let Mentaro schedule difficult cards sooner and confident answers further out. Get a contextual AI explanation when you are stuck.
